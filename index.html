What We Do

Everything Between Booking
and Results Day

You handle the exam content. We take care of the venue, the systems, the people, and the paperwork.

🏢

Venue Hosting

Two fully equipped computer labs with individual workstations, organized seating charts, and high-speed connectivity. Fits 120+ candidates per session.

🛡️

Security and Surveillance

Round-the-clock CCTV, biometric verification at entry, personal belongings lockers, and controlled access to exam zones. No shortcuts on integrity.

👨‍💼

Staffing and Invigilation

We deploy trained invigilators, a registration desk team, and on-site tech support. Everyone is briefed on your specific exam protocol beforehand.

⚡

Uninterrupted Power

Online UPS on every workstation plus a diesel generator on standby. In three years of operations, we have not lost a single exam to a power cut.

🌐

Dual ISP Network

Two independent broadband lines with automatic failover. Managed switches, dedicated bandwidth per seat, and a network admin available during every exam.

📊

Reporting and Compliance

Post-exam attendance sheets, CCTV footage archives, incident reports, and audit-ready documentation. Delivered to your team within 48 hours.
